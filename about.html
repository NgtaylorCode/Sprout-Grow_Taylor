About Sprout & Grow

Sprout & Grow is a greenhouse nursery based in Raleigh, North Carolina, dedicated to helping people grow healthy, sustainable gardens. We specialize in organic plants and provide practical support for gardeners of all experience levels.

🌍 Our Mission

Our mission is to make gardening accessible, rewarding, and environmentally responsible. We believe that anyone can grow plants successfully with the right knowledge, tools, and support.

What Makes Us Different

  • Organically grown, high-quality plants
  • Beginner-friendly guidance and support
  • Focus on sustainable gardening practices
  • Hands-on workshops and community events

✨ Our Story

Sprout & Grow began with a simple goal: to help people feel confident in growing their own plants. What started as a small local greenhouse has grown into a space where the community can learn, connect, and build greener environments together.
